# 👑 Operation: Birthday Queen 🎂
### The Hilarious & Interactive Birthday Clearance Portal

A fun, interactive, and personalized birthday website built for her special day.

---

## 🌟 Interactive Features Included

1. **Stage 1: Identity & Aging Clearance (Gatekeeper)**
   - Radiant Beauty Meter: Interactive slider with hilarious real-time commentary.
   - Spiritual Status Selector: Multiple choice quiz on spiritual vs biological age.
   - Evasive "No, Freeze Time" Button: Playfully runs away and taunts her when hovered or touched!
   - Confetti blast & party horn when unlocked.

2. **Stage 2: The Interactive 3D Bakery & Candle Ceremony**
   - 4 Cake Flavors (Chocolate Truffle, Strawberry Dream, Lavender Matcha, 24K Gold Chaos).
   - Dynamic toppings (Strawberries, Crown, Sparkles, Rubber Ducks, Confetti).
   - Real-time candle flame physics with smoke effects.
   - Blow out the candles using your voice/breath via the **Microphone** or the **Blow Button**!
   - Triggers birthday celebration music, applause, and a universe wish confirmation.

3. **Stage 3: The Roast & Toast Fortune Machine**
   - Cosmic slot machine / fortune dispenser with 10+ hilarious and cute roasts & compliments.

4. **Stage 4: Emergency VIP Birthday Passes (Claimable)**
   - 6 humorous, legally binding redeemable coupons (Argument Immunity, Zero-Calorie Midnight Feasting, 12-Hour Sloth Pass, TV Remote Dictatorship, Pampering Pass, Boba Chauffeur).
   - Clicking "Claim Pass" stamps it with authentic sound effects and confetti!

5. **Stage 5: Balloon Pop Frenzy Mini-Game**
   - Floating interactive balloons drift upwards.
   - Tap/click to pop balloons with pop sound effects, score counter, and level rank promotions!
   - Every pop displays a floating compliment or funny quote.

6. **Stage 6: The Chaos Soundboard**
   - Built-in Web Audio API synthesizer for instant zero-lag sound effects:
     - 🎺 Party Horn
     - 👏 Royal Applause
     - 😱 Soap Opera Drama Gasp
     - 💥 Vine Boom
     - 🔔 Fairy Chimes
     - 🎶 8-Bit Retro Birthday Song
     - 🤸 Cartoon Boing
     - 🔨 Approval Thud

7. **Stage 7: The Heartfelt Letter Envelope**
   - Interactive 3D envelope with a wax seal that breaks upon clicking.
   - Unfolds smoothly to reveal a warm, heartfelt, customizable birthday letter.

8. **Stage 8: Official Aging Certificate**
   - Elegant official certificate declaring her 100% iconic.
   - Clean "Print / Save PDF" feature formatted with `@media print`.

9. **Customization & Sharing**
   - Click **"⚙️ Edit Info"** in the top bar to customize her name, age, nickname, and personal letter.
   - Generates a shareable URL (e.g. `index.html?name=Sophia&age=24&nick=Boba+Queen`) that you can send directly to her phone or WhatsApp!

---

## 🚀 How to Open and Run

- Simply double-click **`index.html`** in this folder to open it in any web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox).
- It works 100% offline (uses the native Web Audio API and HTML5 Canvas with no dependencies).
- Can also be deployed in 1 minute to GitHub Pages, Netlify, or Vercel.
